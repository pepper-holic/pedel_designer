# JRC4558D notch up: 13 18 26 32 / 33 27 19 14. The ground pour is not drawn: the pale pads (22 32 43 45 50 54 55 56 65
# 74 and the GND header 98-101) are ground.
# Clip rotary (common 83 on - in A): BYPASS 71 = 470p to out A, LIGHT 77 = two red LEDs anti-parallel, SILICON 87 =
# 1N4148 pair, GERMANIUM 97 = 1N34A x3 (2:1 - its out-A end, 92/93, runs left along the bottom). Fixed on SILICON
# (JUMP 83 87); the other branches end on an open lug and are left out with their parts (470p, 2 LEDs, 3 1N34A).
# Output DIP toggle 75/84/90 (common 84 = OUT): 90 = the volume wiper, 75 = out B through 10uF/100K (clean) - fixed on
# the effect side (JUMP 84 90); the clean branch stays (it hangs off the buffer with its own 100K to ground).
# Pots have no "1" marks: GAIN 58 wiper (out A) / 59 (- in) / 57 empty, clockwise more gain; TONE 60 (from the 1K) /
# 61-62 tied (10n), clockwise brighter; VOLUME 63 / 64 wiper / 65 ground. The kit lists four 100K pots; the fourth
# has no footprint here.
