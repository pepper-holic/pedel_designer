# Seller packs TL072 for the discontinued LF442CN. Chip silk symbols (notch at the bottom): left + 32 / - 42 / out 44 / V+ 52,
# right V- 33 / + 43 / - 45 / out 53 -> pin 1 = 53, 2 = 45, 3 = 43, 4 = 33, 5 = 32, 6 = 42, 7 = 44, 8 = 52.
# JUMP 38 43: A +in runs under the resistor bodies to the 1M / 47n node. JUMP 32 53: B +in goes round to A out (under the chip).
# JUMP 42 59: B -in runs down under the chip body and drops into the middle of the 20K silk (x~256, between 58 at 210 and 59
# at 308). Read as 59 (the 20K/100K junction, which otherwise has no third lead): B's ground leg is then 20K+100n in series
# (gain 6 above ~80Hz) with 100K setting DC gain 2, and the 20K/10n low-pass after it makes the mid peak. The other choice (58)
# would put 100n straight from -in to Vref (open-ended treble gain), which is not a mid boost.
# JUMP 16 17 25: OUT, the 220K pulldown and the MID wiper share the yellow at y~190 (x~300 drops to the wiper).
# JUMP 20 24: the 220n/10n node runs right then down to the MID lug 24. Pads 10/11 are bare mounting rings.
# Earlier pass (kit-deferred) left this for a Fender mid-boost schematic check; that check is still not done.
