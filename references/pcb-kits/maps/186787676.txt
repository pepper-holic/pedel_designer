# TL072 notch up: pins 1-4 = 24, 28, 36, 41; pins 5-8 = 42, 37, 29, 25. Toggle 3-4-5 (4 = ground
# common): position 3 puts the 1n to ground, position 5 the 2.2n. Normal (centre) = neither; the
# circuit here is the Normal position (both caps left floating via NC).
BOM 1Mx2 100K 47K 22Kx2 4.7K 470n 100n 68p 47uFx2 10uF 1N400X LED-REDx2 TL072 C500K B100K
# (parts list also has the 1n and 2.2n toggle caps)
io IN=38 OUT=17 V9=44 GND=52
JUMP 38 39
NC 3 5 14 11 12 13
# 1n (11-12) and 2.2n (13-14) are the toggle's Warm/Bright caps, out of the circuit in Normal.
