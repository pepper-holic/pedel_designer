# Two THE BLOID circuits (CH1 top, CH2 bottom) on one board; this map is CH1. Shared and written in both maps: 1N400X, the
# 100uF on 9V, Vref 10K/10K + 47uF. Each channel keeps its own six 10K, three 1K, two 100R (kit list 10K x14 = 6+6+2).
# TL072 notch down: 1=65 2=57 3=43 4=30(GND) / 5=29 6=42 7=51 8=64(V+), matching the silk marks.
# TONE: lug 89 on -in B (via 91), wiper 90 -> 100n -> 220R -> GND, lug 92 on +in B (trace runs up to pin 5); THE BLOID's map
# has the third lug empty, this board wires it.
# LED square pad is the anode (kit note): a=41 (-in B), k=56 (out B). DRIVE 96-97-98 with 96 tied to the wiper (rheostat 97-98,
# clockwise raises the feedback resistance), ACCENT 93 (no copper)/94 wiper/95, VOLUME 86 at Vref. Pot names from the silk.
# Jazz/Rock DIP (26 common, 35 ground plane, 8 empty) fixed at Rock: the 470n 7-37 is left out (kit list 470n x4 = 2 per channel).
# CH1 status LED and its resistor (13, 25, 31, 59, 60) are not audio; 99/100 are vias on the 22uF net.
