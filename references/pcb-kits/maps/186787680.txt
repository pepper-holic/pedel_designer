# All three ICs have the notch at the top: pins 1-4 down the left, 5-8 up the right.
# U1 (silk TLC2272, kit ships TL072): 37 40 48 56 / 57 49 41 32. The B half is unused (pin 5 grounded, 6-7 tied) and left out.
# U2 LM386: 33 42 50 58 / 59 51 43 34. U3 LM386: 35 44 52 60 / 61 53 45 36. Pins 1, 7, 8 of both have no
# copper (33 shows a lone ring, no trace), so both chips run at their internal gain of 20.
# Hidden traces (copper tint under the silk, confirmed on the render): TONE lug 3 (pad 3) runs diagonally to the
# 10K/470n node (17); TONE pad 5 runs down past the 10K/41K bodies into the 10n (27).
# FUZZ: "1" by pad 2 (to ground through 100R), lug3 (0) from 9.1K, wiper to U2 pin 3.
# TONE: "1" by pad 3 (the low-passed node). VOL: "1" by the ground pad 8.
# Supply: 9V pad -> 10R -> rail with 100uF and a reverse-protection 1N400X (anode on ground).
