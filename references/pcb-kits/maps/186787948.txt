# TL072 (silk TLC2272) notch up: 48 56 62 71 / 72 63 57 49.
# The extractor read the left 2N7000's outline lines as a thermal "+" and called its centre a ground pad, which merged
# the A stage's - input (2, 9, 14, 53, 55, 56) with ground - SPLIT. The centre pad came out as four fragments
# (17 18 22 23): 22 stands for it. The 22K's bottom pad split the same way (26 27 34 35): 26 stands for it.
# 2N7000s (TO-92 S-G-D facing the flat side): left one flat side right, so bottom 28 = S, centre = G, top 9 = D;
# top one flat side down, so 14 = S, 15 = G, 16 = D. Each is diode-connected (G tied to D) on the side its BAT41s
# block, so only the body diodes conduct: out A -> BAT41 -> BAT41 -> body diode -> - in (3 junctions), and back
# - in -> body diode -> BAT41 -> out A (2 junctions).
# Pots: Sensitivity "1" by pad 2 (the - input), its third lug ring has no copper - a 0-1M rheostat with the 820R in
# the feedback, clockwise = more gain. Accent's "1" lug ring has no copper: wiper 74 to the grounded lug 73 is a
# 0-10K rheostat, clockwise = less resistance in the - leg (more boost). Contour "1" by 79 (to + in B). Level "1"
# by the ground pad 5, wiper to OUT through the cyan.
# The 220n and 47n both sit between the 820R end (16) and the Accent wiper (17) - read as drawn (parallel).
