# Light-purple rings without copper are ground-plane pads (8 12 32 39 58 77 107 117 120); 123-126 are the off-board GND points.
# U1 (top, vertical, "1" at 14): 14 28 33 39 / 15 29 34 40 = pins 1-4 / 8-5. U1A is a follower on Vref with nothing on its output.
# U2 (bottom, notch right): top row 86 85 84 83 = pins 1-4, bottom row 99 100 101 102 = pins 5-8.
# JUMP 86 89: U2A out runs under the NE5532 body silk to the 1uF+ (and the GAIN pot / 1M). JUMP 95 116: 1uF- runs down the
# left edge and along the bottom to the TONE pot lug. JUMP 88 103: TONE wiper diagonal to the VOLUME lug. JUMP 93 48: 2SK209 S
# (cyan up to the 220n pad 93) goes on up to the 10K source resistor. 2SK209 D is the long cyan round the board edge to 9V (3).
# Pots carry no labels; photo GAIN SENS / VOLUME TONE. By role: GAIN = rheostat in U2A feedback (A1M), SENS = attenuator after
# the JFET (330R to ground, 68n lug-wiper), TONE = pot from signal to 68n-to-ground (wiper out), VOLUME = A100K. SENS/TONE between
# A25K and B10K is a guess (SENS A25K audio attenuator, TONE B10K).
