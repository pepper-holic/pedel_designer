# LM386 notch at the top: pin 1 top-left (19), pins 1-4 down the left (19 23 28 35), 5-8 up the right (36 29 24 20).
# Pin 2 and pin 4 sit on the ground pour; pin 7 (bypass, 24) has no copper.
# The extractor ties the wiper (5) - OUT (14) trace into ground (it bends within reach of the grounded lug 6) and the
# pin 1 (19) - 10R (22) - 1uF (47) group too (beside the grounded pin 2): both split out (ring extraction 2026-10-31). Pad 46 (390pF foot) carries the ground
# "+" the small render hides from the extractor.
# SW1/SW2 (41/42) are the on-on gain toggle's two wired lugs: closed puts 1uF across pins 1-8 (gain ~200),
# fixed closed here (the fuzz setting); open leaves the chip at its internal gain of 20.
# SAG: the "1" is printed by pad 3, and the wiper is tied to that lug, so the pot is a 0-10K rheostat in the
# supply after the 9V pad.
BOM 2.2M 1K 47R 10Rx2 100n 390p 47uFx2 22uF 1uF 1N400X LM386N-3 A100K A10K
io IN=26 OUT=14 V9=8 GND=0,46
SPLIT 5 14
SPLIT 19 22 47
JUMP 41 42
NC 24

R 10R 11 12 R5
