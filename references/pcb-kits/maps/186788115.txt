# Upper LM833 (notch left): pins 1-4 along the bottom row 54-57, 5-8 back along the top row 38-35. Its B half (5=Vref
# divider 22K/22K+100uF, 6=7) buffers Vref for both chips. Lower LM833 (notch down): 1=97 2=91 3=86 4=72, 5=71 6=85 7=90 8=96.
# Zeners: 31-32 band at 32 (-in side), 45-46 band at 45 (output side) - anodes meet at 31-46, a back-to-back pair across 220K.
# The bootstrap 10uF (12 +, 3 -) runs from the buffer's 100K/150K base-bias node to its emitter.
# Pots have no silk names; the angle pot row is read by role: 61/62=63 is the C100K rheostat in the gain leg (62-63 tied,
# 61 on -in), lug order set so clockwise lowers the resistance (gain up); 59/60/52 is the A100K level (52 ground, 60 wiper on
# the 220n into the second chip, 59 from the 1K).
# "Buff Out" pad 94 is a second, buffered dry output (emitter -> 10uF -> 100K -> 475R 48-49 -> 94); the app has one output,
# so its 475R is left out here and in the BOM line (kit list 475R x2). The 10uF + 100K stay as the emitter load.
# Pad 70 (100n 69-70) is a ground '+' pad with no drawn copper; 50 is a bare 9V via.
