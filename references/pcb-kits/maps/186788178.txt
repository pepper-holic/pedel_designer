# TL074 notch left: bottom row 87..93 = pins 1..7, top row 77..71 = pins 8..14.

# Pots are on-board angle pots with no labels (49-51, 55-57, 137-139, 140-142, 143/144/146); parts list A1M x2, B1M, A250K, B250K.
#   추측: TREBLE B1M = 140-141-142 (100p from the stage-B coupling node to lug 140, lug 142 on the bass 22n, wiper -> 470K -> +in A) - AC30 top boost.
#   추측: BASS = 144-143-146 (lug 144 on the TREBLE bottom, wiper on the second 22n + 10K, 146 GND) - AC30 top boost bass pot.
