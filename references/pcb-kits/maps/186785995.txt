# No parts-list image on this page. Same circuit as the BIAS [1K] board (186787691), which this
# map was checked against: same parts, same diode directions. The transistor has no pin labels:
# its left pin feeds 2.2M/10K/100n (collector), the middle pin the 47n/2.2M node (base), the right
# pin the BIAS pot (emitter). The Level pot's wiper (L2) goes straight to the OUT jack off the board.
# The seller note "47n 왼쪽의 9V 포인트가 IN" refers to an older silk; electrically pad 20 is IN.
io IN=20 V9=6 GND=24
JUMP 1 14    # base: transistor middle pin to the 2.2M
JUMP 14 22   # 2.2M to the 47n
JUMP 6 19    # 9V to the 10K
JUMP 7 15    # clipping node: 1N400X to the 1N4148
JUMP 10 11   # 100n to Level lug 3
JUMP 11 15

STAGE input input 입력
R 1M 26 27 R1
C 47n 21 22 C1

STAGE gain gain 공통 이미터 (COT50)
Q 2N5089 c=0 b=1 e=2 Q1
R 10K 18 19 R2
R 2.2M 13 14 R3
POT C10K BIAS 5 4 3
C 22uF 16 17 C2

STAGE clip clipping 비대칭 클리핑
C 100n 9 10 C3
D 1N400X a=7 k=8 D1
D 1N4148 a=23 k=15 D2

STAGE output output 출력 (레벨)
POT A100K LEVEL 12 OUT 11
