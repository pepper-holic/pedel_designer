# ICL7660 notch up: 20=1 28=2 31=3(GND) 37=4 / 38=5(VOUT = -9V rail n8) 32=6 29=7 21=8(V+). The 10uF 48-49 is the
# flying cap (dropped by the converter). Positive ground: the 47uF/100uF/100n '+' pads sit on the 0V (ground) side.
# Hidden trace: Ge emitter 10 -> BIAS trimmer / 47uF node 7 under the TO-5 outline. Trimmer lugs 3 and 6 are tied
# (variable resistor from the emitter node to the 1K). Big ring pads 40-41-42 are the off-board A10K volume (lug 40 on
# the collector, wiper 41 to the 10n, lug 42 on the -9V rail). The DIP on-off-on (24/30/35) adds the 22n (44-45) or
# 6.8n (50-51) to the 4.7n; drawn in the centre-off position, so the two caps are left out of the netlist and BOM
# (kit list: 22n x1, 6.8n x1). Status LED pads 53-57 are not in the audio path.
