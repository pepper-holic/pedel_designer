# "ALL RAT" board shared by NEW Cat and NEW Dirty Cat; the variant parts (C13, R4, R10, R15, R16, D1/D2) come from the
# seller's variant table (img_03), not the parts list. R15 and R16 are empty in this variant; the D1/D2 selector
# (56-57-72) is bridged 1-2 for the axial diodes (2-3 is the Turbo LEDs).
# LM308 (notch up): 54=1 61=2 65=3 77=4 / 55=8 62=7 66=6 78=5. The 30p compensation cap on pins 1-8 (45-46) is left out:
# the LM308 model carries its slew/bandwidth itself, and the two comp pins have nothing else on them.
# Pots have no "1" marks: the middle pad is the wiper; DISTORTION and FILTER are rheostats turned so clockwise adds
# resistance (more gain / darker), VOLUME lug1 on the ground pad 43.
# Hidden traces (confirmed on the render): FILTER lug 14 runs down-left to the clipping node (50, the hint shows it
# through the cyan crossing); the JFET source (24) runs under its label to the 10K/C13 node (33); the gate (35) runs
# right to R10 (31); the 1N400X cathode (94) runs up to the filtered 9V (80), which also feeds the 100K Vref divider.
# Checked against the ProCo RAT (ElectroSmash): 2.2M where the original has 1M on the input, the rest connection for
# connection - 22n/1K/1n into + in, 1M bias, 100K+100p feedback, 47R+2.2uF and 560R+4.7uF legs, 1K/4.7uF to the
# clippers, 1.5K + FILTER + 3.3n, 22n into the 2N5459 follower (1M gate, 10K source), C13 to the 100K volume.
