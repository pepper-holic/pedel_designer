# Positive ground: emitters 21/62 and the dark '+' squares 56/67 (electrolytic + ends, no drawn copper) sit on 0V; n5 (ICL7660
# pin 5: pads 5/7/36/63/68/77/84) is the -9V rail. ICL7660 notch left, pins 4 3 2 1 top (50 51 52 53) / 5 6 7 8 bottom
# (68 69 70 71); 10uF 48-58 is the flying cap, pin 1 (53) is tied to V+ on the board. 9V in 55 -> 1N400X 54/72 -> n1 (V+, 100uF 82).
# MKII/MK1.5 DIP (DIP DUAL on-on, columns A 33-38-46 / B 34-39-47, commons 38 = IN, 39 = Q2 base): fixed in the MkII position
# (JUMP 38 46, 39 47) so Q1 is in circuit; the MK1.5 position's 4.7uF 23-24 (IN straight to Q2 base) is left out of the
# netlist and the BOM (4.7uF x3 on the parts list). Centre pads 30/49 are empty. The extractor merged 46/64/73 (Q1 input)
# with 47/61 (Q1 collector 100n) under the DIP - SPLIT (trace_pad: 46 -> 64, 73; 47 -> 61).
# Trimmers: 10K (Q3 collector) lug 2 - wiper 6 tied to lug 3; 100K (Q2 collector) lug 4 - wiper 7 tied to lug 5 (-9V).
# ATTACK B1K rings 40 (100R) / 41 wiper (4.7uF to 0V) / 42 ground; LEVEL A100K rings 43 (10n) / 44 wiper = OUT 25 / 45 ground.
# Two 10n on the board (26-27 output, 60-73 input shunt) for 10n x1 on the parts list. Status LED 8-9 and LED_R 0-1 (470R) are
# not audio and are left out (parts list has a second 470R for it); LED- pad 10 is off-board.
