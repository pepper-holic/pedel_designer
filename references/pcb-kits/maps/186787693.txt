# BS170 (S-G-D from the top as printed): S 9 -> BOOST lug "3" (0), G 14, D at the bottom ring - the extractor split
# it into 15 16 19 20 (19 stands for it). BOOST C5K mini pot: lugs "2" (3, wiper) and "1" (4) on the ground pour,
# so it is a 0-5K source rheostat, clockwise = less resistance = more gain.
# 1N4148s clamp the gate: 7 (ground) -> 17 (gate) and 8 (gate) -> 18 (9V).
BOM 1Mx3 100K 5.1K 100n 47uF 10uF 1N4148x2 BS170 C5K
io IN=5 OUT=36 V9=27 GND=2
NC 6 15 16 20

STAGE input input 입력
R 1M 13 26 R1
C 100n 12 21 C1
D 1N4148 a=7 k=17 D1
D 1N4148 a=8 k=18 D2

STAGE boost gain BS170 공통 소스
Q BS170 g=14 d=19 s=9 Q1
R 1M 10 24 R2
R 1M 25 11 R3
R 5.1K 22 23 R4
POT C5K BOOST 4 3 0

STAGE output output 출력
C 10uF 30 35 C2
R 100K 29 28 R5

STAGE power power 전원
