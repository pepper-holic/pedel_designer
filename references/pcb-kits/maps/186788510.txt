# The 22p between pins 1 and 8 (45-56) is offset-trim compensation and is left out of the netlist and BOM (kit list:
# 22p x1). Hidden traces: Q1 base 75 <- 1M/820K node 88; tone wiper 6 -> volume lug 30; gain wiper 34 tied to lug 35.
# Same board as VOO-DOO BASS (186788511) with pad ids shifted by the 2.2n / 3.3n pads: the bass version leaves the 2.2n,
# 3.3n and 12K (0R jumper) unfitted. Pad ids here = bass id + 2 (ids 2-7) or + 4 (ids 8 and up).
# Both diode chains have their cathodes toward ground as drawn on the silk (same polarity - half-wave clipping).
