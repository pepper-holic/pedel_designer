# JRC386D notch at the top: pins 1-4 down the left (24 30 38 40), 5-8 up the right (41 39 31 25).
# Pins 1 and 8 are tied by a top trace under the notch and go to ground through 47R + 47uF: no gain cap,
# the chip runs flat out. Pin 7 (31) has no copper.
# DRIVE: the silk says A250K but the kit's parts list ships A20K - the parts list is followed here.
# "1" by pad 34: lug1 goes to ground through 4.7K, lug3 (32) takes the input jack straight (no input cap).
# TONE: "1" by pad 4 (the darker node after 1.8K/100n), lug3 on the node after the first 4.7K/22n.
# VOLUME: "1" by the ground lug 37.
# 1N400X across the supply (anode on ground) as reverse protection, 47uF on 9V.
BOM 4.7Kx3 1.8K 330R 47R 100n 47n 22n 390p 47uFx3 1N400X LM386N-3 A20K A100K B50K
io IN=46 OUT=51 V9=50 GND=0
