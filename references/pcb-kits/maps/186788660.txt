# LM833 notch up: 1=29(out A) 2=34 3=45 4=53(GND) / 5=54(+in B) 6=46 7=35(out B) 8=30(18V).
# NE5532 notch up: 1=102(out A) 2=112(-in A, tied to out = buffer) 3=116 4=126 / 5=127(Vref) 6=117 7=113(out B) 8=103(18V).
# Supply: 9V ring 4 -> 1N5817 38-39 -> n10 (100uF 26). ICL7660 doubler: pin 2 drives 10uF 71(+)-78, 1N5817 48->47 and 64->37 make the
#   18V rail (100uF 67). The pump's diodes and cap are not simulated and left out of the BOM line (RAIL 37 18); parts list 1N5817 x4, 10uF x2.
#   Vref = 47K/47K from 18V + 100uF 24.
# The silk at 76-83 says 1N4002 but the parts list has no 1N400X - it is the 4th 1N5817. 92-93 is the 3mm red LED (round, + square = anode).
# Mode DIP on-on 77/84/91 (common 84 = out A): fixed at 77-84 (the 1N4148 pair, silk BA482, across the feedback). 84-91 would put the LED
#   straight from out A to -in. The 1N5817 76->83 and the LED 93->92 stay in circuit beside the 10K either way.
# Pots are rings with no labels (85-87, 88-90, 107/119/136, 108/120/137); parts list A500K, B100K, B10K, B5K.
#   추측: DRIVE A500K = 85-86-87 rheostat (85=86 on out A, 87 -> 10K -> -in A) - TS drive pot.
#   추측: TONE B10K = 137-120-108 (lugs on +in B and -in B, wiper -> 220n -> 220R -> GND) - TS tone.
#   추측: VOLUME = 88-89-90 (lug 88 from the TS stage via 1000n·1K, wiper -> 100n -> Q2 follower, lug 90 Vref).
#   추측: BLEND = 136-119-107 (wiper 119 on Vref, lug 136 on the drive path 15K, lug 107 on the clean path 15K) - clockwise = more drive.
#   후보: VOLUME B100K / BLEND B5K 또는 반대, 지금은 VOLUME=B100K (1K 뒤 팔로워 입력), BLEND=B5K (15K 합산 저항 대비).
