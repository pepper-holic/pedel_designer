# Both ICs lie on their side with the notch on the left: pins 1-4 along the bottom left to right, 5-8 along the
# top right to left. TL072: 31 32 33 34 / 20 19 18 17 (the B half, 18-20, has no copper - left out).
# LM386N-4: 35 36 37 38 / 24 23 22 21. The silk prints generic op-amp symbols on the LM386 footprint; the
# standard LM386 pinout fits the nets (pin 3 and 4 grounded, 6 on V+, 7 to a 47uF, 5 to the 220uF/speaker).
# TL072 A is a follower (pins 1-2 tied).
# Hidden traces: the V+ cyan from TL072 pin 8 (17) runs down past the ⊕ silk into the 100K divider's top (29);
# the LM386 output (24) runs right and down into the 10R Zobel (45). The 220uF supply cap's minus (4) has no
# drawn copper: it sits on the ground pour (the other big pads read the same way), joined to GND.
# VOLUME (angle B1K, the amp's one knob): 25-26-27 in the middle, 27 has no copper - a 0-1K rheostat between
# LM386 pins 1 and 8 (gain ~41 at 1K to ~200 at 0). Turned so clockwise lowers the resistance (louder).
# SW1/SW2 are the rocker power switch, fixed on (JUMP). LED_R (3.3K, not in the kit list) and the LED pad are
# the indicator - left out. Real load is an 8 ohm speaker; the app drives its usual 1M amp input.
