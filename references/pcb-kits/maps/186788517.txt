# BIAS trimmer: wiper 67 tied to lug 60 on Q2's collector, lug 57 to the 1K (rheostat).
# Positive ground: electrolytic '+' pads 24/26 sit on 0V. Kit list has B50K / B1K mini pots that are not fitted.
