# Same layout family as Custard Cake (186788053): one clipper instead of two, the solo stage at x2.5, no output inverter
# (IC3 A unused: pins 1-3 have no copper, NC 106 107 108).
# Off-board solo switch: goods page wiring diagram (img_00; PDS 777 points to it): right footswitch 3PDT pole with P (150)
# common, S (148) solo throw, B (149) blues throw. Fixed in solo mode (JUMP 148 150); blues mode is P to B instead.
# The extractor joined S and B with SOLO's middle lug 8 and the 6.8n/4.7K node 17/18 at the top-left corner where the two
# edge traces run side by side, as on Custard Cake: S runs the outer edge to lug 8, B the inner one to 17/18 (SPLIT 148 8).
# IC2 A buffers P: - in A (91) is a lone pad next to out A (90); Custard Cake ties the same two pins on the board, so
# JUMP 90 91 (follower). The 6.8n on the B node (17-37) returns to the 9V rail (pad 37 sits on the cyan 9V trace) - an AC
# ground through the 100uF.
# TL072 silk: top row V+ / out B / - in B / + in B, bottom row out A / - in A / + in A / GND (pins 8 7 6 5 / 1 2 3 4).
# Pots: "1" marks on the right lug. DRIVE and TONE wipers tied to lug 1 (rheostats); SOLO's middle lug is S (divider from
# the boosted signal on lug 3 to a 10K to Vref on lug 1); VOLUME lug 1 on ground, wiper to OUT.
