# Two identical King of Tone channels side by side; this map is the left one (B side reuses the same layout, shifted). The
# 1N400X and the 100n on the protected 9V are shared and written in both maps; each channel has its own 47R, Vref 47K/47K and
# two 100uF.
# 4580D notch down: 1=162 2=147 3=137 4=125, 5=124 6=136 7=146 8=161. B half (+ from the 22n/1M input) is the gain stage, A half
# (+ at Vref) the inverting stage.
# The extractor ran B's output trace (146) under the 100p body into the -in net: SPLIT keeps out B = DRIVE wiper 1, pin 7 and
# the 100p's lower pad 160; -in B is 136, 135, 44 (100K), 70 (toggle), 181/189 (27K/33K legs).
# Toggles (3-pad DIP on-on rows, each using two pads): Soft 71-85 puts the BA282 strings from out A through 6.8K to -in A;
# Hard 72-86 puts the 1N914 pair from the 1K/TONE node to Vref; the third 52-70 puts 1K across the 100K drive resistor (lower
# gain). Fixed here: Soft on, Hard off, 1K off - the 1N914 pair and the 1K (24-25) are left out of the netlist and BOM line.
# Pots read by role: DRIVE 0-1-2 (wiper 1 = out B, lug 2 -> 100K feedback so clockwise raises gain, lug 0 -> 10K forward),
# VOLUME 7-8-9 (lug 9 at Vref), TONE 150-151-152 (152 -> 10n to Vref, 150 from the 1K). PRESENCE trim wiper is the apex pad 149.
BOM 1Mx3 220K 100K 47Kx2 33K 27K 10Kx2 6.8Kx2 1K 47R 1000n 100nx2 22n 10nx4 100pF 100uFx2 1uF 1N400X BA282x4 JRC4580D A100K B100K B25K
