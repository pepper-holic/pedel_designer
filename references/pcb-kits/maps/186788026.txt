# Transistor pins top to bottom: C (19), B (23), E (33). All three pots have "1" on the right.
BOM 2.2M 47K 680R 100n 47n 10n 47uF 1N400X 1N914x2 2N3904 B100Kx3
io IN=16 OUT=34 V9=20 GND=31
JUMP 6 16    # IN to the GAIN pot

STAGE input input 입력 (GAIN)
POT B100K GAIN 8 7 6
C 100n 15 30 C1

STAGE gain gain 공통 이미터
Q 2N3904 c=19 b=23 e=33 Q1
R 2.2M 13 35 R1
R 47K 28 29 R2
R 680R 14 36 R3

STAGE clip clipping 다이오드 클리핑
C 47n 22 21 C2
D 1N914 a=24 k=9 D1
D 1N914 a=10 k=25 D2

STAGE output output 출력 (볼륨·톤)
POT B100K VOL 2 1 0
POT B100K TONE 5 4 3
C 10n 12 11 C3

STAGE power power 전원
C 47uF 18 17 C4
D 1N400X a=26 k=27 D3
