# SEC RET PRE (186788334): Chase Tone Secret Preamp (Echoplex EP-3 preamp). One SMD 2N5457 common-source stage on a
# 22V rail from an ICL7660 pump. No seller resource for the toggle or volume wiring (pedalparts PDS board searched,
# 2026-10-31): both are decided by circuit logic below.
# JFET: the SMD body has no ring pads; its legs are the cyan traces under the S/G/D silk, so the Q line names a pad of each
# leg's net: S = 3.6K/10n/22n node (pad 2), G = 22n/1M node (pad 25), D = 100n/50K trimmer node (pad 17).
# Supply: 9V ring 7 -> 1N400X 14-34 -> n14 (100uF 54, ICL7660 pins 1 and 8). Pin 2 (50) pumps the 10uF 55-64 and 57-66
# through four 1N400X (43-44, 37-38, 45-46, 39-40, 10uF 56-65 to ground) - a Dickson tripler clamped by the 22V zener
# (59-60) and the 100uF 58. Pins 3 and 5 are on ground (no inverter rail). The pump is not simulated: RAIL 58 22, the
# pump diodes/caps and the zener are left out here and on the BOM line (parts list: 1N400X 5, 10uF 3, 22V zener 1).
# The page says the drain sits near 11V with the trimmer at noon.
# EQ toggle (on-off-on, rings 0/8/20): the middle lug 8 is tied to the ground pad 9 (light-purple '+' pad), lug 0 takes
# the 10n and lug 20 the 22n source caps, each with a 1M to ground (3-4, 9-10) against pops. Fixed with the source bypass
# on: JUMP 0 8 = 10n across the 3.6K (the brightest of the three modes; centre = no bypass, 20 = 22n). The toggle shorts
# the 1M 3-4 in this position, so it is left out (BOM 1Mx2 here, 1Mx3 on the parts list); lug 20 is the open position.
# Volume A500K (angle pot 47/48/49): the middle lug 48 has no copper, so it is the wiper wired off the board. Lug 49 is
# the 100n/240K output node, lug 47 runs to a 110K into OUT, and a second 110K joins 49 to OUT. The only reading that
# makes a volume is wiper -> ground: CW (wiper toward 47) grounds the 47 side and leaves 49's 110K to OUT (about -6dB),
# CCW shorts the output node (silence). Lug 1 = 49 so that clockwise is louder.
# Silk reads 220pF for the input shunt cap; the parts list has 22pF (and no 220p), so 22pF is used.
