# Pots are off-board along the top edge; the product photo reads LOW CUT, GAIN TRIM, LEVEL LOW, LEVEL HIGH from the front, so
# inside (board) left to right: 1-2-3 LEVEL HIGH (wiper -> HIGH pad), 4-5-6 LEVEL LOW (wiper on the transformer's driven side),
# 8-9-10 GAIN TRIM (rheostat 9-10 in the Q2 collector -> Q1 emitter feedback, 8 empty), 11-12-13 LOW CUT (rheostat 12-13 from
# the Q2 emitter to the 330uF bypass, 11 empty). Values from the kit list by role (estimate): A10K for LEVEL HIGH (after the
# 600R transformer), A250K for LEVEL LOW, B25K for GAIN TRIM and LOW CUT. Rheostats run so clockwise raises gain / cuts more lows.
# Trimmers have the wiper at the apex pad (55, 95), tied to one lug.
# Toggle fixed at 9V: its common (7) to the protected 9V (0); the 24V branch - ICL7660 and the 4x 1N400X + 4x 10uF multiplier
# (44-47/79-82, 98-101/109-112, IC 48-84) - is left out here and in the BOM line (kit list ICL7660, 1N400X x5, 10uF x5).
# The left footswitch board (A type, wiring not published) is fixed in the HIGH position: OUT (124) takes HIGH (122), SW (123)
# grounds TRIM HIGH (assumed: the HIGH channel adds that emitter bypass). The right board is the bypass: IN 128, OUT 129.
# Q2 base has two joined pads (74, 75). LED resistors (85-118, 86-119) and LED pads are status only.
# TV-141P: driven winding 38-78, the other 37-77 (centre taps 56/57 empty); 1:1 so the phase is not marked.
