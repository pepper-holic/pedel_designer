# The J201 is a SMD part on a TO-92 footprint (18 drain, 19 source, 20 gate - read from the nets: gate to IN
# and the 2.2M, source to 1.5K + 22uF, drain to the trimmer and the 10n). The --smd re-extraction turned pot
# outlines into virtual pads here, so the plain extraction (52 pads) is used.
# LM386 notch at the bottom: pin 1 bottom-right (38), pins 1-4 up the right (38 28 23 12), 5-8 down the left
# (11 22 27 37). Pin 7 (27) has no copper. The 22uF across pins 1-8: its + pad (45) runs under the cyan 9V
# trace into pin 1 (38) - the crossing is not tinted, so JUMP (the extractor's hint to the 9V net is the cyan).
# BIAS trimmer: pads 39 and 43 sit on the 9V net, 30 on the drain - a 0-50K drain resistor.
# GAIN: "1" by the ground pad 8, lug3 (6) from the J201 via 10n, wiper through 10K to pin 3.
# VOLUME: "1" by the ground pad 2. TONE: lug3 (3) from the volume wiper, lug1 (5) to 10n to ground, wiper = OUT.
BOM 2.2M 10K 1.5K 100n 10nx2 22p 47uF 22uFx2 1N400X B50K LM386N-1 J201 A1M A250K B100K
io IN=49 OUT=26 V9=48 GND=2
JUMP 38 45
NC 27

STAGE input input 입력
R 2.2M 32 51 R1

STAGE jfet gain J201 공통 소스
Q J201 g=20 d=18 s=19 Q1
POT B50K BIAS 30 39 43
R 1.5K 31 50 R2
C 22uF 36 35 C1

STAGE gain custom GAIN (입력 감쇠)
C 10n 17 16 C2
POT A1M GAIN 8 7 6
R 10K 25 24 R3
C 22p 13 29 C3

STAGE amp gain LM386 (이득 200)
IC LM386N-1 1=38 2=28 3=23 4=12 5=11 6=22 8=37 U1
C 22uF 45 44 C4

STAGE output output 출력 (볼륨·톤)
C 100n 10 9 C5
POT A250K VOLUME 2 1 0
POT B100K TONE 5 4 3
C 10n 15 14 C6

STAGE power power 전원
D 1N400X a=42 k=21 D1
C 47uF 34 33 C7
