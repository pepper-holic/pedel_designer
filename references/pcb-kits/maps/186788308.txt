# ZEN 84 = 2N7000 (diode-connected, D/G on out A) -> 1N34A, and back ZEN -> 2N7000 -> 1N34A -> out A, DUM 93 = no
# clipper. Fixed on ZEN (JUMP 78 84); the MRSL LEDs and the open DUM lug are left out (as on Duel A) - the LEDs and
# the indicator LED/470R are not in the BOM line below.
# Vertical angle pots, no "1" marks: GAIN 48 (out A) / 49 wiper (10K to out A) / 50 (- in), turned so clockwise adds
# resistance to - in; VOICE 51 (from the 1K) / 52 wiper (100n to Vref) / 53 empty; TONE is the ring column 65 (from
# the 10K) / 79 wiper (+ in B, 3.3n) / 87 empty - clockwise brighter; VOLUME 54 / 55 wiper OUT / 56 ground.
# The 100uF's + (20) is reached by a cyan diagonal that leaves the 1N400X body: on the protected rail (JUMP 20 12).
