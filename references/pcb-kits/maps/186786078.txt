# The red-filled 47n box at the left edge hides its two pads (the extractor finds neither); its top pad joins 61 (10K from IN) and
# its bottom pad runs to Q1's base 85, so it is written on 61 and 85. The output 470R's left pad 82 runs under its red body to
# OUT (91/92) - JUMP 82 91.
# Pots ("1" on the right lug of each): GAIN B250K lug1 12 (out A), wiper 11 tied to 10 (47K to -in A): more feedback R clockwise;
# BASS B50K lug1 4 (out B side) - wiper 1 - 0 (out A side); TREBLE B50K lug1 3 (out B side) - wiper 2 - 5; LEVEL A100K lug1 15 (Vref)
