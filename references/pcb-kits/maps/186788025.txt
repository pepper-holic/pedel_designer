# ICs (pin 1 from the notch): TL072-1 notch down (1=43 .. 4=20, 5=19 .. 8=42) is the input buffer (A) and the BLEND buffer (B);
# TL072-2 notch right (1=17 .. 4=14 top row right to left, 5=36 .. 8=39 bottom row) is the CMOS-path output buffer (A) and the
# preamp (B); TL072-4 notch left; TL072-5 notch up; TL072-6 notch right. CD4049UBE notch right: 1=66 .. 8=59 along the top,
# 9=83 .. 16=90 along the bottom. Inverters 9->10, 11->12, 14->15 are used; inputs 3/5/7 are tied to 9V with outputs 2/4/6 open
# (they stamp nothing), 13/16 are NC.
# TL072-4A has no feedback: an open-loop comparator on the 470K/15K input. Its output feeds the 1N914 pair (105->106 direct,
# 96<-97 through 470K) into 1n and the B follower.
# The two Sallen-Key cells: 10K/33K with 1n to the TL072-5B output and 1n to ground at its + input; the second (10K, 33K into
# TL072-6A +) has its ground 1n on the first node, as drawn.
# The rail is n18 (9V after the 1N400X); Vref is 10K/10K + 100uF. Pads 67-70 are vias on the 9V net. Pots read with lug 1 at the
# silk "1" (right end): LEVEL lug 1 at Vref (AC ground) confirms it. BLEND lug 3 is the dry buffer, lug 1 the LEVEL wiper.
