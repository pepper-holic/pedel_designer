# Off-board solo switch: the goods page wiring diagram (img_00; PDS 777, the build log, points to it) wires the right
# footswitch's 3PDT with one pole on S/B/P: P (166) is the common, S (164) the solo throw, B (165) the blues throw; the other
# pole switches the SLO/BLU LEDs. Fixed in solo mode (JUMP 164 166) so the SOLO knob is live; blues mode is P to B instead.
# The extractor joined S and B (and with them SOLO's wiper 8 and the 3.3n/4.7K node 19/20) where the two edge traces run
# side by side at the top-left corner; the flood from each pad shows S runs the outer edge to SOLO's middle lug 8 and B the
# inner one to 19/20, so SPLIT 164 8.
# IC3 (right): the extractor also joined out B (66) and - in B (67); the flood splits them into {66, 32, 37, 69} and
# {67, 33, 36, 96, 98}, which puts the 100K and 68p across the stage as its feedback (SPLIT 66 32 37 69). IC2 A really is
# a follower (pins 1 and 2 tied on the board): it buffers P (1M to Vref) into the tone/mix stage.
# TL072 silk: top row V+ / out B / - in B / + in B, bottom row out A / - in A / + in A / GND (pins 8 7 6 5 / 1 2 3 4).
# Pots: "1" marks on the right lug. DRIVE and TONE have the wiper tied to lug 1 (rheostats); SOLO's middle lug is S, a
# divider between the clipped signal (lug 3) and a 10K to Vref (lug 1); VOLUME lug 1 on ground, wiper to OUT.
