# Checked against the standard (negative-ground, NPN silicon) Fuzz Face: 33K Q1 collector load,
# Q1 collector straight into Q2 base, 100K from Q2 emitter back to Q1 base, Q2 emitter through the
# 1K fuzz pot to ground with 22uF from the wiper, output from the 330R/8.2K junction through 10n
# into a 500K audio volume - all as on the board. Kit adds a 1M input pulldown; 330R where the
# original has 470R.
BOM 2.2uF 22uF 10n 1M 100K 33K 8.2K 330R BC183 BC183C B1K A500K
io IN=18 OUT=13 V9=6 GND=29

STAGE input input 입력
C 2.2uF 8 15
R 1M 19 20

STAGE core fuzz 퍼즈 코어 (Q1·Q2 직결)
Q BC183 e=21 b=24 c=25 Q1
R 33K 9 10
Q BC183C e=26 b=27 c=28 Q2   # the kit page lists Q2 as BC183C (high-gain grade)
R 330R 11 12
R 8.2K 16 17

STAGE feedback custom 피드백 (Q2 이미터 → Q1 베이스)
R 100K 30 31

STAGE fuzz custom 퍼즈 (Q2 이미터 바이패스)
POT B1K FUZZ 2 1 0
C 22uF 7 14
