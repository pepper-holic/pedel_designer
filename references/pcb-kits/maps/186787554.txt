# Discrete op-amp (Spark Mini style): Q1 (+in from the 100n/1K input, biased by 22K/22K Vref through 100K) and Q3 (-in = GAIN lug 1)
# share emitters (n12) fed by the Q4/Q5 current source (Q4 base 100K from V+, 1K emitter sensed by Q5). Q2 (2N3906) is the
# PNP voltage stage: base 1K from Q1's collector, 100p Miller cap, 1K load to ground; its collector (n1) is the output node.
# GAIN B500K silk "1" on the right = pad 3 (Q3 base); wiper 2 is tied to lug 1 (n1) so the pot is a feedback rheostat
# (clockwise = more gain) with 100p across it and the 10K + 1uF ground leg on Q3's base. 4.7uF 62-69 sits between the emitter
# node (62) and Vref (69, '+') per trace_pad. 1N400X band at 72: cathode on the filtered V+ (n7), anode to ground (shunt protection).
# Transistor pins follow the silk C/B/E (top to bottom); Q1 22/29/40, Q2 23/30/42, Q3 24/31/44, Q4 56/58/70, Q5 53/67/71.
BOM 1M 100Kx4 22Kx2 10K 1Kx5 100R 47R 100nx2 1n 100pFx2 220uF 10uF 4.7uF 1uF 1N400X 2N3906 2N3904x4 B500K
io IN=0 OUT=85 V9=84 GND=10
