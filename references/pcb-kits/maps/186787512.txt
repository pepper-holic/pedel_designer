# Left TL072 notch up: 12 23 27 32 / 33 28 24 13. Right TL072 notch down: pin 1 bottom-right, 51 40 31 26 / 25 30 39 50.
# FREQ is a dual A100K, each gang wired "1" + "2=3" (a rheostat): gang A 0 -> 1 (to 4.7K -> Vref), gang B 6 -> 7. The
# cyan from 0 runs through 7 and on to the 22n (10) and U2 pin 5 (25) - JUMP 7 10. The two gangs are two knobs here.
# GAIN "1" by pad 2 (U2A out), pads 8-9 tied: a 0-100K rheostat in U2A's feedback, clockwise = more gain.
# 12dB: "1" by pad 5 (the input buffer), lug3 (3) on U2B out, wiper into U1B + in.
BOM 1Mx2 200K 47Kx2 10Kx5 6.8Kx2 4.7Kx2 56n 22nx2 100uF 47uF 1uF 1N400X TL072x2 A100Kx3 B10K
io IN=60 OUT=70 V9=66 GND=42
JUMP 7 10
JUMP 8 9
