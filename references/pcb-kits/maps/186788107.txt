# Both ICs are TL072 (seller: the left one's "4558D" silk is a misprint). Left notch: 65=1 74=2 83=3 93=4 / 66=8 75=7 84=6 94=5;

# 10uF 28-29 / 30-31 makes +18V on 24/31/66/72/118 (both op-amps' V+, top of the 10K/10K Vref). The pump, its two diodes and two
# caps are not simulated and left out of the BOM line (RAIL 66 18). Vref 47uF 91-80: its minus pad 80 shows no copper, wired to ground.
# U1A is a unity buffer for the "Buff Out" jack (4.7uF 32-25, 100K, 470R 33-11 -> pad 10); that jack is off-board, so the 470R
# that only reaches it is left out (470R x3 on the list).
# Angle A100K pots, unlabelled (mounted under the board), assigned by net: 44/45-46 sits between the 1K from U2A's output and U2A's
# -in -> GAIN (wiper 45 tied to 44); 15-16/17 between the clipper node and the 1.5K/3.3n low pass -> FILTER (wiper 16 tied to 17);
# 49-50-51 after the output 470R, wiper 50 = OUT, 51 ground -> VOLUME.
